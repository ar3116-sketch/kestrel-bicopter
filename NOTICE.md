# Attribution and project status

Kestrel Mini R4 is an independent hobby prototype. The Black Hawk name describes styling inspiration; no manufacturer endorsement is implied. No general open-source license has been selected for this repository.

`source/motor_*.jpg` and the EMAX servo images are manufacturer reference material. `source/bluebird_BMS115_dimension.png` is a manufacturer drawing linked from the Blue Bird servo catalog. `source/matek.html` is a saved Matek specification page. Their underlying rights remain with their owners; inclusion documents the design inputs and does not grant reuse rights. See the linked references in the engineering review and `docs/references.json`.

`source/bf_servos.c` and `source/bf_target.config` are third-party Betaflight source/configuration snapshots. Preserve their source notices and consult the upstream repository license for redistribution and reuse. Pinned revisions are recorded alongside them; no firmware binary is provided here.

Local CAD screenshots depict the project itself. Historical user screenshots, unrelated original Samson files and local application state are not part of this repository.
